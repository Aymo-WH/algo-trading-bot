# PRE-REGISTRATION — Gordian v2 Design (FROZEN upon operator approval)

Frozen: 2026-07-07. Full rationale: `research/design.md` (same commit).
After approval, this file is immutable — changes require a new spec file plus a
logged operator approval. Phase 0 hooks will block edits mechanically.

## Quarantine boundary (Prime Directive scope)
- Train/validation: panel start (~2000) → **2021-12-31**
- **Quarantined holdout: 2022-01-01 → 2026-06-30** — no strategy performance may be
  computed/viewed on this slice until the one-shot, operator-token-gated `final_eval.py`.
- Purge 5 trading days, embargo 10 trading days at every train/test boundary.

## Universe rule (fixed; no return-based membership)
69 ETFs = audited tickers with ≥$10M median 3y daily dollar volume
(list: `research/data_recon/universe_summary.csv` minus TUR, FXE, EWM, EZA, DBA, FXY,
EWS, RSX). Point-in-time entry at 252 days of history; panel starts at first weekly
rebalance with ≥20 eligible names.

## Target
5-trading-day forward return, cross-sectionally demeaned (primary); beta-residualized
variant as robustness check. Weekly rebalance, Wednesdays.

## Signal graduation rule (Tier 1: S1 XS-momentum blend, S2 TS-trend, S3 low-beta, S4 seasonality)
On train/validation walk-forward: mean weekly rank IC ≥ 0.01 AND Newey-West t ≥ 2.0
AND positive-IC share ≥ 60% of calendar years. Fail any leg → dropped.
Tier 2 (carry) requires new data sources and prior operator awareness.

## Model admission rules
M0 = equal-weight z-composite baseline (mandatory). M1 (XGBoost) admitted iff
CPCV-median net Sharpe ≥ M0 + 0.1 and PBO not worse. M2 (PPO sizing) same rule vs M1.

## Validation gates (unchanged from FABLE_MISSION.md §2)
Net Sharpe ≥ 0.5 · majority of sub-periods positive · MaxDD ≤ 20% · PBO < 0.5 pass
(≤ 0.2 target) · DSR > 0.95 with true trial count · seed/fold/perturbation stability ·
survives 10 bps/side (5 baseline, 20 stress).

## Canaries (all must pass; a trip halts work)
Within-date label shuffle · row-block time shuffle · feature time-shift ±26w ·
synthetic null panel (must find nothing) · planted-signal panel (must recover it).

## Trial budget & kill criteria
≤ 250 logged trials for Phases 2–5 (discards/ablations count; DSR uses true count).
K1 no Tier-1 signal graduates → try Tier-2, else null. K2 book net Sharpe < 0.2 on
walk-forward at budget → null. K3 PBO > 0.5 → not holdout-eligible. K4 tripped canary
→ halt. Audit triggers: net Sharpe > 1.2 (audit) / > 1.5 (presumed bug).
